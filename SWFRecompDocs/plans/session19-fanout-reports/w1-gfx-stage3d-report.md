## 1. What exists today, and the premise refuted

**Refuted: "maybe one missing present/composite step flips many."** There is
nothing to present. `SWFModernRuntime/src/avm2/avm2_stage3d.c` is, by explicit
design (shader3d-arc.md tranche S1/S2), a *validating no-op surface*: it
retains **no** renderable state anywhere.

- `context3d_draw_triangles` (line 775) is 4 lines: null-check `indexBuffer`, return undefined.
- `avm2_builtin_add_method(ctx, c3d, "present", s3d_noop)` (line 2772) — literally `s3d_noop`.
- `context3d_clear` ignores all seven parameters; `setScissorRectangle` ignores its rectangle.
- `Avm2Buffer3DExt` stores `{context3d, count, data32_per_vertex}` — **uploadFromVector/ByteArray store no data**.
- `Avm2Program3DExt` stores `{context3d, uploaded}` — the validated AGAL **bytecode is discarded** after `agal_validate`.
- `Avm2Texture3DExt` stores `{w, h, is_cube, format flags}` — no texels.
- `setProgramConstantsFrom*` validate and drop; `setVertexBufferAt`, `setCulling`, `setDepthTest`, `setBlendFactors`, `setStencilActions`, `setSamplerStateAt`, `setRenderToTexture` are enum validators returning undefined.

So the answer to brief Q1 is: **a whole backend is missing**, not a final step.
A `drawTriangles` call ends at that 4-line validator. Confirmed on the pixel
side: every failing actual PNG is a single flat colour = the stage background
(`stage3d_triangle*` → pure `(0,255,0)`; `stage3d_blend`/`sampler`/`raytrace` →
pure white; `stage3d_fractal`/`bitmap` → pure black).

What *does* exist and is reusable: the AGAL **parser/validator**
(`agal_validate`, avm2_stage3d.c:1009-1130 — header, 24-byte tokens, register
types, sampler-config extraction, the #36xx error map), profile negotiation,
deferred context creation at the end-of-frame slot, and `driverInfo =
"Dummy Ruffle driver"` (already byte-matching: `stage3d_texture`'s actual PNG
shows that text rendered correctly over the blank 3D area).

## 3. How Ruffle does it (brief Q2), and the minimum architecture

- `render/naga-agal/` (2 195 Rust lines: `builder.rs` 1 806, `types.rs` 247, `varying.rs` 142) parses AGAL and **builds a naga IR module directly** (no WGSL text).
- `render/wgpu/src/context3d/` (2 145 lines: `mod.rs` 1 286 command processor, `current_pipeline.rs` 699 state→`RenderPipeline` cache, `shader_pair.rs` 160 LRU of compiled modules).
- `core/src/avm2/object/context3d_object.rs` (548) records `Context3DCommand`s; `present()` **swaps front/back** colour+depth+resolve views and resets the pending-clear flag.
- Composite: `display_object/stage.rs:625-634` — *"All of our Stage3D instances get rendered **underneath** the main stage"*, in `stage3Ds` order, each translated by `Stage3D.x/y`; `surface/commands.rs:866` scales the quad by the texture size and draws it with the **`bitmap_opaque`** pipeline (no blending, no smoothing).
- `configureBackBuffer(w,h,antiAlias,…)` → `sample_count = antiAlias` rounded **down** to a power of two, then clamped by adapter support (`mod.rs:522-560`); 4 is what the corpus asks for.
- Constants: `vc[128]` vertex / `fc[28]` fragment `vec4f` uniform arrays at `@group(0) @binding(0|1)`; 8 vertex attributes; 8 textures; 8 temporaries (AGAL1) / 26 (AGAL2).

**Minimum architecture to render a triangle** (either backend): retain program
bytecode → translate/interpret AGAL → retain vertex/index buffers + attribute
bindings + constants → rasterise into an offscreen colour buffer with the
configured sample count → swap on `present()` → composite the front buffer as
an opaque nearest-sampled quad at `(Stage3D.x, Stage3D.y)` **before** the
display-list walk.

Injection point in our tree is exactly one line:
`avm2_display.c::avm2_render_walk` (19460) — after `renderer_open_pass`,
before `avm2_render_node(ctx, ctx->stage, …)`. AS code and the render walk never
interleave (`avm2_main.c`: `avm2_display_run_tick` then `avm2_render_frame` /
`avm2_render_present`), so Stage3D commands can be executed and submitted
during the tick with their own encoder without touching the main pass.

## 4. The measured finding: the golden is exactly reproducible, and cheaply

`<scratchpad>/w1-gfx-stage3d/raster.py` — a ~40-line NumPy rasteriser for
`avm2/stage3d_triangle` (5 vertices, 2 triangles, `mov`-only shaders, clear
`0.3,0.3,0.3`, 320×200 back buffer, `antiAlias = 4`) reproduces the upstream
golden with **max channel diff 1 and ZERO channels above tolerance 1** over the
whole 320×200 back-buffer region (517 of 192 000 channels differ by exactly 1).
That test's check is `tolerance = 1, max_outliers = 0` → **it would pass**.

The model that achieves this, all four parts confirmed against golden pixels:

1. Composite at `(0,0)` **unscaled and opaque**, back buffer occupying exactly (0,0)-(319,199) of the 550×400 stage; everything outside is stage green.
2. **Standard Vulkan/D3D 4× sample positions** `(0.375,0.125) (0.875,0.375) (0.125,0.625) (0.625,0.875)`. Worked example: golden pixel (0,0) is `(38,38,102)` = 2 of 4 samples covered, the covered ones carrying the top-left vertex colour `(0,0,128)` and the other two the clear `76` → `(0+0+76+76)/4 = 38`, `(128+128+76+76)/4 = 102`. Solving the edge `y = 0.625x` against the four sample offsets gives exactly 2 inside.
3. Fragment attributes are evaluated at the **pixel centre even when the centre lies outside the primitive** (extrapolation). This was the single biggest error in my first pass: masking to the centre-inside case left 280 wrong edge pixels with diffs up to 127; removing the mask took the whole image to max-diff 1.
4. Per-sample `round(clamp(x,0,1)*255)` store, resolve = `floor(mean of 4 samples)`. (`0.3f*255 = 76.5 → 76`; the 2-white/2-clear pixel `165.5 → 165`.)

Composite filtering independently confirmed on `stage_scale_factor`
(`scale_factor = 2.0`): the golden's 2×2 blocks are constant at even
coordinates → **nearest**, block-aligned, matching Ruffle's `bitmap_opaque`.

Two consequences:

- Our CI and Ruffle's CI both grade on **lavapipe** (`ruffle/.github/workflows/test_rust.yml:19` installs `mesa-vulkan-drivers`; our `verify_output.py:4273` forces `VK_ICD_FILENAMES=…/lvp_icd.json`). A GPU implementation inherits the golden's own rasteriser → parity by construction.
- A **CPU** implementation is *also* viable at these tolerances, now measured rather than assumed.

## 5. Pricing the slices (brief Q4)

### Route choice (this is the coordinator's decision, both are real)

**Route GPU** — new TU `SWFModernRuntime/src/rendering/render_stage3d.c` +
`include/rendering/render_stage3d.h`; AGAL→**WGSL text** emitter (Dawn takes
WGSL; Ruffle's naga IR path has no C analogue), pipeline/shader-module cache,
`WGPUBuffer` vertex/index/uniforms, MSAA colour + resolve + depth/stencil
textures, per-`drawTriangles` render pass, front/back swap, plus a new
`bitmap_opaque`-equivalent pipeline in `render_webgpu.c` for the composite.
~1 600-1 900 LOC for slice A. Files touched: new TU, `render_webgpu.c`
(+pipeline, +`render_webgpu_draw_stage3d_texture`), `render_webgpu.h`,
`avm2_stage3d.c` (state retention + command recording), `avm2_display.c`
(composite call), `verify_output.py` source list, `CMakeLists.txt`, the
Emscripten build script.

**Route CPU** — AGAL *interpreter* (a switch over vec4 ops, ~250 LOC; no string
emission, no shader compiler, no pipeline cache) + a software rasteriser
implementing §4's model (~450 LOC: edge functions, 4× sample mask, centre
extrapolation, scissor, cull, depth, stencil, blend, colour mask) + composite
through the **existing** `render_webgpu_draw_bitmap_quad` (~20 LOC, no renderer
change at all). ~750 LOC for slice A, zero new GPU pipelines, so near-zero
regression blast radius on the other 550 comparisons, and it works unchanged in
the browser build. Its ceiling is G6: an interpreter at ~200 AGAL ops × 220 k px
× 80 frames (`raytrace`) is minutes-to-hours, and `away3d` is worse.

Recommendation: **if the goal is corpus comparisons, CPU-first** (A+A′ = 15
comparisons for ~1 000 LOC, then G4 needs a texture sampler, ~300 more);
**if the goal is the Stage3D feature** (Starling/Away3D titles in the browser —
`project_avm2_direction`, `feature-priority-map` §9), only the GPU route pays,
and the CPU rasteriser is then still worth keeping as a byte-level oracle for
debugging. Writing both is the outcome to avoid deciding into by accident.

## 6. Refutations and caveats (attacking my own pricing)

1. **"22 comparisons" is not a flip count for one change.** They decompose into
   five mechanism groups; no single slice takes more than 13.
2. **`stage3d_bitmap` (the board's #5 row, 85 740 excess) is NOT a clean
   Stage3D flip.** Its `test.toml` carries `with_default_font = true` (the toml
   itself says *"TODO Fix this test. It shouldn't depend on the default font"*)
   and its budget is `tolerance 4 / max_outliers 24` — 24 channels total. Our
   actual PNG is **uniformly black**, i.e. we are missing something *besides*
   Stage3D there (the overlay text Ruffle draws in the top band is absent from
   our render too). Price it as B-plus-an-unknown, not as B.
3. **`away3d` is not a Stage3D row so much as an engine row** — Away3D 4 with
   cube maps, render-to-texture, ~1 863 distinct colours in its text band, and
   the same `with_default_font` dependency at `tolerance 30 / max_outliers 400`.
   HOLD; completion mechanism = A+A′+B+B′ landed and green, *then* re-measure.
4. **`stage3d_raytrace`**: 80 frames of a fragment-heavy OGSL-generated shader
   at `tolerance 8 / max_outliers 10`. Under the GPU route this is plausible
   (same lavapipe shader backend); under the CPU route it is a performance
   NO-GO. Its `sin`/`log` results are hardware-transcendental — under CPU they
   would have to match lavapipe's polynomial approximations to 8/255, which I
   did **not** test. HOLD either way until A lands.
5. **Five of the 29 comparisons are `misc`-group** (`stage3d/*`): invisible to
   `categories=all`. Any wave-2 ledger for them must be graded on a
   `categories=full` run.
6. **`png_unavailable` ≠ unknown mechanism**: those 5 `stage3d`-suite rows are
   blank for the same reason, just without published PNGs (their JSON is still
   from s18's run `33945288707`). Their excess (591 k-660 k on 550×400) is
   consistent with whole-buffer blankness.
7. **Trace-axis risk of implementing this**: all 29 tests currently PASS on
   trace (`avm2/stage3d_*` and `stage3d/*` are 5/5 + 7/7 green since
   `dfbbfc1af`). A backend that starts throwing real GPU errors — several
   fixtures set `enableErrorChecking = true` (`stage_scale_factor`) — can
   *regress* trace rows. Keep every existing validator, and add no new throw.
8. **`stage3d_blend`'s 1.0 M excess overstates its ripeness**: it is 1 300×810
   with a 6×8 grid; it needs G2 *and* G4, so it is not in slice A despite being
   the board's largest row.

## 7. Vendoring / licence (brief Q5)

**Vendoring naga-agal is not realistic; transliterating it is, and is already
precedented in this file.** Ruffle's render crates are MIT OR Apache-2.0 and
have zero `ruffle_core` deps, but `naga-agal` emits **naga IR**, not WGSL, and
the standing ruling (`ruffle-code-reuse-ruled-out`, user decision 2026-07-29)
rules out linking their crates — the wasm/Emscripten wall and the retained-vs-
immediate boundary mismatch are unchanged here. The ruling's own carve-out
("port Ruffle's MIT WGSL shaders, keep attribution") plus the two arcs already
shipped on this pattern — S2's `agal_validate` is a hand transliteration of
`parse_bytecode` + `extract_sampler_configs`, and PixelBender P1/P2 transliterated
their PBJ parser and used `naga-pixelbender` as the opcode-semantics spec — make
a C AGAL→WGSL (or AGAL interpreter) written **from `builder.rs` as the spec**
the obvious and licence-clean route. Cite `builder.rs` line ranges per opcode in
comments, exactly as `avm2_stage3d.c` already does.

## 8. The two `blank_render × Video / NetStream` rows (brief's "also glance")

`avm2/netstream_play_flv` (229 724 excess) and `avm2/netstream_seek_flv`
(40 672). **Neither is dispositioned on the pixel axis** — the
`ACCEPTED_DIFFS.md` entry for the avm2 test exists *explicitly to say it is
not* (`<!-- image-axis: none -->`, "NOT dispositioned (blank render, live
work)"). **Neither is cheap, and neither is a graphics row**: both also fail on
**trace** (0/16 and 0/49 — wave0 trace inventory lines 52/54), and
`tests/swfs/avm2/ignored_tests.txt` already carries the measured diagnosis:
they need the NetStream *playback state machine* (`Play.Start`/`Buffer.Full`/
`Seek.Notify`…) before any frame can be decoded. Our AVM2 `Video` is a stub —
`avm2_display.c:16861` registers `attachNetStream` as `video_noop` — while the
AVM1 side only ever decodes the **first** FLV frame as a stamp
(`action.c::flv_decode_first_frame`), so there is no timed-playback machinery to
reuse, only the codec layer (`video_codec.c`, libavcodec, which already has
FLV1/VP6/H.264 wired). Owner: an AVM2 NetStream arc; the trace rows are the
cheaper gate and must come first.
